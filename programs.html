Year-round programming.

Maths Clinic

Free tutoring editions helping partner-school teens close learning gaps in a subject that too often decides their future.

Post-UTME Scholarship

Fully sponsored Post-UTME coaching at Extramural, Agbowo, Ibadan, with free accommodation, for female Okeho Muslim indigenes scoring 200+ in UTME. The overall best candidate wins a full university scholarship.
