04 Grounded In Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

Empowering Women, Enriching Lives

OFMA is dedicated to uniting Okeho Muslim women to inspire personal and collective growth rooted in faith. Through education, advocacy, and service, we empower women to reach their fullest potential, fostering a strong connection to our Deen, unity, and positive community impact.

Vision

Transforming Lives, Building Bridges

OFMA envisions confident, dedicated Muslim women actively shaping a harmonious society in alignment with the Deen — a beacon of unity, understanding, and cooperation, building a legacy of empowerment, education, and sisterhood for generations.
